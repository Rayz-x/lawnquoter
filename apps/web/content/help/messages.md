## Templates

Office roles open Messages. Crews do not edit customer messages. The page says "Crews do not edit customer messages."

1. Pick a channel, email or text.
2. Pick a purpose: Quote, Invoice, On my way, Reminder, Follow-up, Abandoned quote, or General.
3. Write the subject (email) and the body.
4. You can use these tokens: customer name, company, link, amount, and address. They fill in when the message sends.
5. Save the template. One template can be the default for that purpose.

## Texting

Texts stay off until Twilio is connected. The message is "Not connected - add Twilio keys to enable".

On my way, from a visit, texts the customer that the crew is coming. It uses the same Twilio connection.

Follow-ups and the sending domain are separate settings. The sending domain stays off until Resend is connected: "Not connected - add Resend key to enable".

Call tracking stays off until CallRail is connected: "Not connected - add CallRail key to enable".
