## Templates

Office roles open Messages. Crews do not edit customer messages. The page says "Crews do not edit customer messages."

1. Pick a channel, email or text.
2. Pick a purpose: Quote, Invoice, On my way, Reminder, Follow-up, Abandoned quote, or General.
3. Write the subject (email) and the body.
4. You can use these tokens: customer name, company, link, amount, and address. They fill in when the message sends.
5. Save the template. One template can be the default for that purpose.

## Texting

Texts stay off until Twilio is connected. The message is "Not connected - add Twilio keys to enable".

On my way, from a visit, texts the customer that the crew is coming. The crew lead sends that text. It uses the same Twilio connection.

## Texting registration

Open Settings, Texting. Carriers review the business before texts go out.

1. The steps are Business, Brand, and Campaign.
2. An owner or admin fills in the legal name, EIN, website, business email, and a sample text, then chooses Save draft.
3. When Twilio is connected and the draft can be sent, choose Submit to carriers.
4. If it was already sent, the page says "This registration was already submitted to the carriers."

Crews see "Crews do not register texting."

## Follow-ups

Open Follow-ups. After a quote is sent, with no reply and no view:

1. Day 2: text the customer.
2. Day 5: email the customer.
3. Then create a call task. A reply, a view, or an acceptance stops the sequence and does not create that call.

The page says "Follow-ups are on." or "Follow-ups are off." An owner or admin chooses Turn on or Turn off.

Crews see "Crews do not run follow-ups."

The sending domain stays off until Resend is connected: "Not connected - add Resend key to enable".

Call tracking stays off until CallRail is connected: "Not connected - add CallRail key to enable".
