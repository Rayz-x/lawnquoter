## Set the fee

1. Open Settings, then Payments, and find Late fees.
2. Leave both amounts at zero to keep late fees off. The card then says "Late fees are off."
3. Flat amount is a fixed dollar fee.
4. Percent of the open balance is a percent of what is still unpaid.
5. Grace days is how many days after the due date to wait, from 0 to 365.
6. Choose Save late fees. The page says "Late fees saved".

You can use a flat amount, a percent, or both. They are added together.

## Add the month

Choose Add this month's late fees.

- The page says "No late fees this month" when nothing is past the grace days.
- Otherwise it says how many late-fee drafts it created.

Each late fee is its own draft. The original invoice total stays as issued.
