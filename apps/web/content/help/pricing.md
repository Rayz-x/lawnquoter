## Price list

Owners and admins open Services & Pricing.

1. Add a service, or add starter services from the catalog. Catalog services land on a draft price list.
2. Starter prices are national planning benchmarks, not your market. The page says: "Starter prices are national planning benchmarks from published cost guides, not your market. Review every price before you activate."
3. Choose Activate price list when the draft is the list you want quotes to use.
4. Choose Raise prices to raise or lower every dollar on the active list by a percent, from -50 to 100.

A service can also have its own price floor. That floor cannot go below the company minimum.

## Units on the price list

The measured quantity is what each price tier counts. Lawn area uses square feet, edging uses feet, and some services use hours or a count of items. The column heading shows that unit. A bigger job uses the next tier, and anything past the last tier uses the extra price.

## Minimum and the first cut

Property adjustments apply to the price list.

- Minimum charge per visit is the lowest price a visit can be.
- Minimum visit length, under More options, is the shortest time the schedule allows.
- First cut of the season costs more adds a percent for overgrown grass on the first visit.

## Slope, gate, obstacles, and stairs

Turn a switch on before its amounts count.

- Hilly yards cost more adds a percent for a gentle, moderate, or steep slope. More options can also add crew time for that slope.
- Narrow gate charge is for gates too narrow for a riding mower. Set the width in inches and the dollars to add.
- Charge for obstacles adds dollars and minutes for each thing the crew mows around.
- Charge for stairs adds dollars and minutes when the crew carries equipment up steps.

The property page is where you record the slope, gate, obstacles, and stairs for a lawn. This page is where you set what those facts cost.

## Drive and a dense route

Trip charge by drive time and distance uses the closest zone that fits the drive you know.

- Nearby is the short trip.
- Town is the longer trip that is still in town.
- Farther is anything beyond town.

Discount stops on a dense route takes a percent off when enough other properties are already nearby. Set how many stops, how close they must be, and the percent to take off.

## Season

Seasonal price change adds or takes off a percent by the month the quote is priced. Set Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter. A negative percent lowers the price.

## Other ways to price a service

A service rule can be a tier table, a season program, snow pricing, a price per sprinkler zone, cost plus markup, or a price built from labor and materials. Pick the rule that matches how you sell that service, then fill the amounts. The page shows an example price as you edit.
