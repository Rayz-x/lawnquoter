## The list

Open Invoices. Filter by Draft, Issued, Paid, or Void.

A numbered invoice shows as INV- followed by the number. A draft with no number shows as Draft.

## Create a batch

1. Set From and To. They begin as the current month.
2. Choose Create invoices.
3. A visit is included when it is completed, has an after photo, is not already billed, is not on billing hold, and was not finished after a cancel.
4. Agreements on level billing are left out of this batch. Those are billed as a monthly amount, not from each visit.
5. If that period has nothing new to bill, the page says "No new billable visits in that period."
6. Otherwise it says "Drafted N invoice(s) from M visit(s)." The new invoices are drafts. Review them, then issue the ones you want the customer to pay.

## One bill for every property

On a business customer, turn on Consolidated invoicing. One bill covers every property for the month. Set that under + Billing details on the customer.
