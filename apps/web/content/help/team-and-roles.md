## Invite a teammate

1. Open Team from the account menu.
2. Type their email and pick a role. You cannot invite another owner from this form.
3. Choose Send invite. The page says "Invitation sent".
4. They get an email and join only after they accept. Until then they show under Pending invites, with an expiry date.
5. Choose revoke on a pending invite to cancel it. No membership exists until the invite is accepted.

There is no per-seat fee. Add the whole crew.

## What each role can do

- **Owner.** Opens every office page, including Import customers, the price book, Team, and the lead form. Only an owner can start the LawnQuoter plan.
- **Admin.** Changes company settings, pricing, the team, and the export. The lead form and the plan checkout stay with the owner.
- **Sales.** Writes quotes and bids, and opens clients, the schedule, money, and reports.
- **Dispatcher.** Runs the schedule and crews, and opens clients, money, and reports.
- **Crew lead and crew.** Open Today, accounts, properties, and the crew phone app.
- **Read only.** Opens office pages such as the schedule and invoices, and cannot change company settings, pricing, or the team.

Owners, admins, dispatchers, crew leads, and crew can also open the crew phone app.

## Change or remove someone

1. On Team, owners and admins see Member actions on each member row.
2. Change the role there, or choose Disable access. A disabled person can be turned back on with Re-enable access.
3. Your own row and the owner row have no actions control.
