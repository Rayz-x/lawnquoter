## What the visitor does

1. They type an address and a ZIP. The ZIP is checked against the area you serve. Outside that area, the page says "This address is outside our service area, so we can't price it here."
2. They pick a service, and answer whether there is a gate and whether there are dogs.
3. They measure the lot on the map, or wait while the lot is looked up.
4. They add a name and a phone or email, then choose See my range.
5. The range appears. A deposit can lock it when Stripe is connected. Until then the deposit line says "Not connected — add a STRIPE_SECRET_KEY to enable".
6. After a deposit, the page says "Deposit received. We'll confirm within 24 hours."

## Paste it on your site

Owners open Settings, Lead form. The card is Hosted form.

1. Hosted link is a page you can send when you do not want to embed the form.
2. Button snippet adds a button that opens the request form.
3. Embed snippet shows the request form on the page.
4. Instant quote embed lets a visitor get a price on the page.
5. Choose Copy. The page says "Copied".

Attribution such as UTM tags comes from the form page URL.
