## Today's stops

1. Open Crew App from the account menu, or go to the crew phone app.
2. The list is today's stops. Finished stops stay collapsed. Choose Show to see them, and Hide to fold them again.
3. Only the crew lead starts and finishes a stop. Anyone else sees "The crew lead starts and finishes stops."
4. Start job starts the clock. Pause stops it. Resume starts it again.
5. The stop needs an after photo before it can finish. Until that photo is taken, the button says "Take an after photo to finish".
6. Finish job asks if the stop is done or partly done. A partly done stop needs a reason and an after photo.
7. Problem lets you skip a stop you cannot finish. Cannot finish tells the office.
8. Upsell sends the office a request, with a photo if you have one.

If another crew already finished the stop, the screen says so and you do not start it again.

## Photos

The stop says "After photo required". Before is optional. Take after photo is required to finish. Photos stay on the phone until they upload.

## When the phone is offline

Changes wait in an outbox: job started, paused, finished, skipped, photos, and time corrections. They send when the phone is back online. Open the outbox to see what is still waiting.

## PIN

The first time, set a PIN on the phone. Later, the app asks for that PIN before it opens the day's stops.
