## Activate a snow day

1. On the schedule, open Snow event.
2. Type the inches that fell. Agreements whose trigger depth is at or under that number get a visit.
3. Choose Activate snow.
4. The page says how many snow visits were scheduled, or "No new snow visits" when every matching agreement is already on that day.

The crew logs materials and times on the visit. One visit is scheduled for each snow agreement that is not already on the day.
