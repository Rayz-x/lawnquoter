## Connect Stripe

Customer payments stay off until Stripe is connected. Money goes straight to your Stripe account.

1. Open Settings, then Payments.
2. The badge says Not connected, Pending, or Ready.
3. An owner chooses Connect Stripe. Stripe may ask for more details. The badge then says Pending, and the card says "Stripe needs a few more details before you can take payments." Choose Continue setup.
4. When the badge says Ready, payouts show On or Off, and the account shows its last four digits. Open Stripe dashboard from the same card.
5. Anyone who is not the owner sees "Only the company owner can start Stripe Connect."

## Card on file

On the customer, Card on file lists saved cards and bank accounts.

1. Until Stripe is connected, the card says "Not connected - add a Stripe key to enable." or "Connect Stripe in Settings before a card can be saved."
2. Choose the button to send the customer through Stripe to save a card. The default card is marked Default.
3. With no card yet, the card says "No card on file yet."

## Charge after a visit

Owners and admins can turn on Charge this card after each visit.

1. The customer needs a default card.
2. The switch uses that card once a visit invoice is issued.
3. Only an owner or an admin can change it. Anyone else sees "Only an owner or admin can change auto-charge."
