## Open a property

Properties lists every service site. A site with a current measurement shows a confidence percent. A site without one shows Needs measure.

Open a property to see the map, quotes, jobs, and visits. Nearby lists other properties within 800 meters.

## Measure

1. Choose Measure on the property.
2. Draw the lawn on the map and save the measurement.
3. The lot size can come from the parcel. A mark next to the lot means it was filled in for you.
4. Choose Re-measure later from the map actions. Re-geocode looks up the pin again.

If you edit the address, type it as Street, City, ST ZIP. Any other shape shows "Enter the address as Street, City, ST ZIP."

## What changes the price

Mowing conditions on the property change the price.

1. Set Slope to Flat, Gentle, Moderate, or Steep.
2. Set Narrowest gate to Under 48 in, 48 in+, or No gate. Under 48 in is push-mower only. 48 in+ fits a rider. No gate is open access. Leave every chip off when you do not know yet.
3. Choose Exact inches for a width from 12 to 240.
4. Set Obstacles for things the crew mows around. Stairs to the turf means the crew carries equipment up or down.
5. Drive from the shop, in minutes and miles, is the trip from your shop to this property.

## Dogs, gate code, and notes

1. Turn on Dogs on site to warn the crew. The property shows Dog on site.
2. The gate code is hidden until you choose Show gate code. Crews see it on visit day only.
3. Access notes are what the customer should know about getting onto the property.
4. Crew notes are for the crew. The customer does not see them.

## Route zone

Type a Route zone to group this property with a route. The box suggests zones you have already used.
