## Log an application

On a crew visit, fill the chemical log before you leave the stop.

1. Type the product name.
2. Type the rate you applied.
3. Type the EPA registration number.
4. Type the temperature in degrees Fahrenheit and the wind in miles per hour.
5. Weather is optional. Use it for a short note such as clear or overcast.
6. Save the log on the visit.

The office can read the log with the visit. It is the record of what went on the lawn that day.
