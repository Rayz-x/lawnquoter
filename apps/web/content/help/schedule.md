## Open the day

1. Open Schedule. The page dispatches a day by crew.
2. Pick the day. Each crew is a column of stops.
3. Drag a scheduled stop onto a crew, or use Move up and Move down to change the order on that truck.

## Stop order

1. Reorder by drive time uses a free estimate. It shows when the crew has at least two scheduled stops.
2. Use paid routing shows when paid routing is connected. It orders those stops with the routing service.
3. Publish route sends that crew's stop order to their phones. It shows while the route is still a draft. Add a visit before publishing an empty route.

When paid routing is off, the page says "Paid routing is not connected. Add ROUTING_API_KEY to enable it. Drive times use the free estimate."

## Rain day

1. Check the stops that should move, or leave them all unchecked to move every stop.
2. Choose Rain day. The button names the next workday.
3. Selected stops, or every stop, move to that day. Stops already in progress can stay put.
4. Choose Clear selection to uncheck the stops you picked.

The page reports how many were pushed, how many were skipped, and how many were left in progress.

## Needs attention

The tray on the schedule lists Unassigned, Overdue, Needs reschedule, and Return visits and flags. When it is clear, it says "Nothing needs attention · Dispatch looks clear".

From a visit in the tray you can assign it to a crew, reschedule it, skip it, or cancel it. A same-day extra can be approved from the tray.
