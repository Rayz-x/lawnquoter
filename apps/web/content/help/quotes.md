## Start a quote

1. Choose New, or open Quotes and start one there.
2. Pick the account. That is the customer this quote is written for.
3. Pick the property, or leave None yet until you add a lawn.
4. Choose Open builder.
5. If no account is selected, the page says "Pick an account".

## What the customer reads

On the quote, set the title, a customer intro, and customer notes. Those show on the proposal. Internal notes stay in the office.

Deposit is the amount collected before the work starts. Expires is the last day the customer can accept this price.

## Send the proposal

1. On a draft, choose Send proposal.
2. The page says "Proposal link ready" and copies the link.
3. After it has been sent, the same button says Copy proposal link.
4. When you change a sent quote, start a revision. The page says "Revision started. Send it again when the changes are ready." Then choose Send proposal again.

## After they accept

Convert to job turns an accepted quote into work on the schedule.

1. Pick one-time work, or a recurring visit.
2. For a recurring visit, pick Weekly, Every two weeks, or Monthly, and the Preferred day.
3. Set the start date and choose Convert to job.

Split an accepted quote into separate jobs. Each job needs a name. That name is what shows on the schedule.

## Bill part of the work

Progress billing drafts an invoice from the lines you check.

1. Check the lines to bill.
2. Set Percent complete from 1 to 100. That is how much of the checked lines to bill now.
3. Choose Draft progress invoice.
