## Save a rule

1. On the schedule, open Weather.
2. Pick the condition: Rain, Snow, Wind, or Freeze.
3. Pick the action. Skip takes the stops off this day. Push moves them to the next open day.
4. Type the threshold. That is how much has to happen before the rule runs. Rain and snow are inches, wind is miles per hour, and freeze is degrees Fahrenheit. A freeze rule runs when the temperature is at or below the threshold.
5. Choose Save rule. The page says "Weather rule saved". Saving the rule does not change today's stops.

With no rules, the card says "No weather rules yet."

## Run it on a day

1. Type today's rain inches, snow inches, wind, and temperature.
2. The button previews the match: Skip this day, Push this day, or No rule matches.
3. Choose that button. The page says how many stops were pushed, skipped, or left.

The first matching rule wins, in this order: rain, snow, wind, freeze. Skip and push do not combine. A reading under every threshold changes nothing.
