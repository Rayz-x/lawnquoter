## Company

1. Sign in. The setup wizard opens before the office.
2. Type the company name. Customers see it on quotes and invoices.
3. Check the URL handle. It is the short name in your public quote pages and the instant-quote link. Letters, numbers, and hyphens only.
4. Choose Create company. If that handle is already used, the page says "That URL handle is taken. Pick another."
5. LawnQuoter saves the timezone from the computer you are using.

## Brand color

1. On the Branding step, type a color such as #1E5235.
2. Choose Save color. Buttons and headings on quotes use this color.
3. You can change the logo and color later under Settings, Branding.

## Starter services

1. Weekly mowing and spring cleanup start checked. Each row shows a typical price.
2. Uncheck any service you do not sell.
3. Choose Add services to put them on a price list and turn that list on.
4. Choose Skip if you want to add services later from Services & Pricing.

## Invite someone

1. On the Team step, type an email, or leave it blank.
2. Pick Crew or Sales. Crew sees visits. Sales can write quotes.
3. Choose Send invite, or Skip. They get an email and join only after they accept.
4. More roles are on the Team page. See [Team and roles](/help/team-and-roles).

## Service area

1. Type the ZIP codes you serve, separated by commas, such as 37064, 37067.
2. Leave the box blank if you are not ready to limit the lead form.
3. Choose Finish setup. You land on Today.

## After setup

Open Settings when you want to change the office address, timezone, currency, or mowing season. Visit times and the mowing season use the timezone clock. Owners and admins can save those settings.
