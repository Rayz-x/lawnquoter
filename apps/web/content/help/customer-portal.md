## What the link shows

The portal link from the customer page lasts 14 days. The customer sees your company name and theirs, then:

- Sites and properties. An empty site says "No properties yet."
- Quotes. An empty list says "No quotes to review." A quote they can sign says Sign. A signed quote says Signed.
- Invoices. An empty list says "No invoices to pay." An open invoice says Pay.
- Upcoming visits, or "No upcoming visits."
- Visit photos, or "No visit photos yet."
- Card on file. Update card opens Stripe when a card can be saved.

## Request service

1. Pick a property, or leave Any site.
2. Type what they want done on the next visit.
3. Choose Send request.

The request shows on the portal and lands in your office requests.
